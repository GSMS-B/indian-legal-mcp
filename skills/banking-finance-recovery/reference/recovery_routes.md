# Comparative Debt Recovery Routes in India

| Forum / Route | Statute | Minimum Threshold | Key Forum / Tribunal | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cheque Bounce** | `NIA` (Sec 138) | Any cheque amount | Judicial Magistrate (JMFC / MM) | 1–3 Years (Criminal/Quasi-criminal) |
| **SARFAESI** | `SARFAESI` | Outstanding debt ₹1 Lakh+, and default > 20% | Self-executing (CMM/DM under Sec 14) | 60-day notice + possession |
| **DRT Recovery** | `RDBA` | ₹20 Lakhs | Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) | 1–2 Years |
| **Insolvency (CIRP)** | `IBC` | ₹1 Crore | National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) | 180 to 330 Days |
