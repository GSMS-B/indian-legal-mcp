# Banking & Financial Regulatory Framework

- **RBI Act:** Section 45-IA requires registration of Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).
- **Banking Regulation Act:** Section 35A empowers RBI to issue mandatory directions to banks.
- **Payment and Settlement Systems Act (PSSA):** Section 4 requires prior authorization from RBI to operate any payment system (wallets, payment gateways, UPI networks).
- **FCRA:** Foreign contributions require mandatory registration with Ministry of Home Affairs and designated SBI New Delhi main branch account.
