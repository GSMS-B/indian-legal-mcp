# Direct Tax Transition Note (Income-tax Act, 2025)

## Enactment & Enforcement
The Income-tax Act, 2025 modernizes and replaces the six-decade-old Income-tax Act, 1961:
- **Total Sections:** 536 sections organized into clean Roman-numeral chapters.
- **Application:** Governs Assessment Year 2026-27 and future assessment years.
- **Prior Assessments:** Assessments for Assessment Year 2025-26 and prior years remain governed by the provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961.
