# GST & Customs Key Provisions

- **CGST Section 7:** Comprehensive definition of 'Supply'.
- **CGST Section 16:** Four mandatory conditions for claiming Input Tax Credit (possession of invoice, receipt of goods/services, tax paid to government, return filed).
- **Customs Act Section 12:** Dutiable goods charging section.
- **Customs Act Section 14:** Transaction valuation rules for imported goods.
