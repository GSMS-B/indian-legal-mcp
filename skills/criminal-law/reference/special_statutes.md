# Overriding Penal Statutes (Lex Specialis)

When evaluating criminal charges, the following specialized enactments override general BNS/BNSS codes:

| Statute | Act Code | Key Overriding Feature |
|---|:---:|---|
| **PMLA** | `PMLA` | Section 45: Twin conditions for bail (public prosecutor given opportunity + reasonable grounds to believe accused is not guilty). |
| **NDPS** | `NDPS` | Section 37: Stringent limitations on granting bail for commercial quantities; reverse burden of proof under Sections 35 & 54. |
| **UAPA** | `UAPA` | Section 43D(5): Complete bar on bail if court is satisfied that allegations are prima facie true. |
| **POCSO** | `POCSO` | Presumption of guilt under Sections 29 & 30; mandatory child-friendly in-camera recording of testimony. |
| **SC/ST Act**| `SCST` | Section 18: Bar on anticipatory bail (Section 438 CrPC / Section 482 BNSS) where prima facie offence is disclosed. |
