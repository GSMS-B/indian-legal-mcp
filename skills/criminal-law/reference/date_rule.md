# The Date of Offence Rule (July 1, 2024 Cut-Off)

## General Principle
Under Article 20(1) of the Constitution of India, no person can be convicted of any offence except for violation of a law in force at the time of the commission of the act.

## Operational Rule:
1. **Date of Offence Prior to 01-07-2024:**
   - FIR registered under **IPC** (Indian Penal Code, 1860).
   - Trial and evidence governed by transitional provisions.
2. **Date of Offence On or After 01-07-2024:**
   - FIR registered under **BNS** (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023).
   - Procedure strictly under **BNSS** (Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023).
   - Admissibility of evidence under **BSA** (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023).
3. **Continuing Offences:**
   - If an offence spans across the cut-off date (e.g., criminal conspiracy commencing in June 2024 and terminating in August 2024), charges are framed under BNS with IPC transitional references.
