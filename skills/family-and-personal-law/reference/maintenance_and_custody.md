# Maintenance & Custody Multi-Statute Channels

In India, a wife or dependent may claim maintenance through multiple overlapping statutory channels:
1. **HMA Section 24 & 25:** Pendente lite and permanent alimony during matrimonial litigation.
2. **HAMA Section 18:** Hindu wife's entitlement to live separately and claim maintenance.
3. **DV Act Section 20 & 22:** Emergency monetary relief and compensation orders for domestic violence.
4. **MWPRDA Section 3:** Reasonable and fair provision and maintenance within Iddat period.
