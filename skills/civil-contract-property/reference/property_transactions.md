# Immovable Property Formalities & Traps

## 1. Transfer of Property Act (TPA) Requirements
- **Sale of Immovable Property (Section 54):** If value exceeds ₹100, can only be made by a registered instrument.
- **Mortgage (Section 58):** Simple mortgage, mortgage by conditional sale, and English mortgage require registered instrument attested by at least two witnesses.
- **Gift of Immovable Property (Section 123):** Must be effected by registered instrument signed by donor and attested by minimum two witnesses.

## 2. Registration Act (Section 17 vs 49)
- Section 17 mandatorily requires registration for all non-testamentary instruments affecting immovable property valued at ₹100+.
- **Section 49 Consequence:** An unregistered document that requires compulsory registration cannot affect any immovable property or be received as evidence of any transaction affecting such property.
