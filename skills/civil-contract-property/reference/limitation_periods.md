# Limitation Periods Quick Reference (Limitation Act, 1963)

| Nature of Suit / Application | Prescribed Period | Starting Point of Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Breach of Contract** | 3 Years | When the contract is broken or breach occurs. |
| **Recovery of Money** | 3 Years | When the loan is made or money becomes payable. |
| **Specific Performance of Contract** | 3 Years | Date fixed for performance, or when plaintiff has notice that performance is refused. |
| **Possession of Immovable Property (Title)** | 12 Years | When possession of defendant becomes adverse to plaintiff. |
| **Tort / Compensation for Injury** | 1 to 3 Years | When injury or damage occurs. |
| **Appeal to High Court from Decree** | 90 Days | Date of the decree or order appealed from. |
| **Appeal to Other Courts** | 30 Days | Date of decree or order. |
| **Execution of Decree (Other than Injunction)** | 12 Years | When decree becomes enforceable. |
