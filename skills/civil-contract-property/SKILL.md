## 5-Step Execution Workflow
1. **Analyze Cause of Action & Subject Matter:**
   - For contracts and commercial promises, verify enforceability under `ICA` (Section 10 essentials, Section 23 legality of object).
   - If remedy sought is specific performance or mandatory/prohibitory injunction, check `SRA` (Sections 10, 14, 16, 20).
2. **Check the Limitation Period:**
   - Every civil remedy is barred if not brought within time. Consult `reference/limitation_periods.md` and check `LA` (Limitation Act Section 3 & Schedule).
3. **Verify Immovable Property Conveyancing Formalities:**
   - If immovable property is transferred, verify `TPA` Section 54 (sale), Section 58 (mortgage), or Section 105 (lease).
   - Check `REGISTRATION` Section 17 (compulsory registration) vs Section 49 (inadmissibility of unregistered documents affecting immovable property).
4. **Determine Jurisdiction & Procedural Forum:**
   - Consult `CPC` Section 9 (civil court jurisdiction), Section 15-20 (place of suing), and relevant Order/Rule for pleadings, summary suits (Order 37), or temporary injunctions (Order 39).
5. **Synthesize Findings & Draft Analysis:**
   - Cite exact statutory sections and quote the operative statutory text.
