# Securities & Capital Markets Overview

- **SEBI Act, 1992:** Section 11B grants powers to issue directions; Section 12A prohibits manipulative and deceptive devices and insider trading.
- **SCRA, 1956:** Governs recognition of stock exchanges, clearing corporations, contracts in securities, and title demutualisation.
- **SAT (Securities Appellate Tribunal):** Hears appeals against decisions of SEBI, IRDAI, and PFRDA.
