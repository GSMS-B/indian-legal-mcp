# Entity Governance Comparison

| Feature | Company (Companies Act, 2013) | LLP (LLP Act, 2008) | Partnership (IPA, 1932) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legal Personality** | Separate legal entity | Separate legal entity | Not a separate legal entity |
| **Liability** | Limited to unpaid share capital | Limited to agreed contribution | Unlimited joint & several liability |
| **Minimum Members** | 2 (Pvt) / 7 (Public) / 1 (OPC) | 2 Designated Partners | 2 Partners |
| **Audit Requirement** | Mandatory for all companies | Mandatory if turnover > ₹40L or contribution > ₹25L | Optional unless tax audit applies |
| **Governing Regulator**| MCA / RoC | MCA / RoC | Registrar of Firms |
