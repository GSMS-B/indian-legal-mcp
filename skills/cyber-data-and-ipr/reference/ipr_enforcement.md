# Intellectual Property Enforcement Guide

- **Copyright Act Section 51:** Infringement occurs when any person does anything the exclusive right to do which is conferred upon the owner.
- **Trade Marks Act Section 29:** Infringement of registered trademark by use of identical/deceptively similar mark.
- **Patents Act Section 3(d):** Mere discovery of a new form of a known substance which does not result in the enhancement of the known efficacy is not patentable.
- **Patents Act Section 48:** Exclusive rights conferred upon a patentee to prevent third parties from making, using, or selling the invention.
