# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)

- **Section 4:** Ground for processing personal data (consent or certain legitimate uses).
- **Section 6:** Requirement of explicit, informed consent with itemized notice.
- **Section 8:** Obligations of Data Fiduciaries (data erasure upon purpose completion, reasonable security safeguards).
- **Section 9:** Additional safeguards for processing personal data of children.
- **Section 18-20:** Establishment and adjudicatory powers of the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Penalties:** Up to ₹250 Crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach.
