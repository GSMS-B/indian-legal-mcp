# Administrative Justice & Citizen Rights

- **RTI Act Section 8(1):** Exhaustive categories of information exempt from disclosure (national security, commercial confidence, cabinet papers, fiduciary relationship, personal privacy).
- **RPA Section 8:** Mandatory disqualification of legislators convicted of specified offences and sentenced to imprisonment.
- **RTE Act Section 12(1)(c):** Mandates 25% reservation in entry-level classes of non-minority private unaided schools for disadvantaged groups.
- **RPWDA Section 34:** Mandates 4% reservation in government establishments for persons with benchmark disabilities.
