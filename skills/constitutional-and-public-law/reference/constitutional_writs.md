# Constitutional Writs & Judicial Review (Articles 32 & 226)

| Writ Name | Nature of Writ | Primary Grounds for Issuance |
|---|---|---|
| **Habeas Corpus** | Produce the body | Unlawful or arbitrary physical detention without statutory authority. |
| **Mandamus** | We command | Compelling a public authority to perform a mandatory statutory duty. |
| **Certiorari** | To be certified | Quashing an illegal order passed by a subordinate court or tribunal without jurisdiction. |
| **Prohibition** | We forbid | Restraining a tribunal or inferior court from continuing proceedings exceeding jurisdiction. |
| **Quo Warranto** | By what authority | Challenging an illegal occupant of a substantive public office. |
