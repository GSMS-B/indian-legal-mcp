## 5-Step Execution Workflow
1. **Identify the Sectoral Regulatory Authority:**
   - Environmental: SPCB / CPCB / Ministry of Environment / NGT.
   - Food Safety: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI).
   - Drugs & Cosmetics: CDSCO / State Drug Controllers.
   - Power & Electricity: Central/State Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC/SERC).
   - Consumer Complaints: District/State/National Consumer Commissions.
2. **Inspect the Statutory Licensing / Consent Requirements:**
   - Under `WATER` (Sec 25) & `AIR` (Sec 21): Mandatory Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO).
   - Under `FSSA` Section 31: Mandatory license or registration for all food business operators.
   - Under `DCA` Section 18: Prohibition on manufacturing/sale of substandard or spurious drugs.
3. **Verify Environmental Principles:**
   - Polluter Pays Principle and Precautionary Principle applied mandatorily by NGT under `NGT` Section 20.
4. **Evaluate Patient & Consumer Rights Protections:**
   - Under `MHA` Section 18: Every person with mental illness has right to access mental healthcare.
   - Under `CPA` Section 35: Procedure for filing consumer complaint for deficiency in service or defective goods.
5. **Synthesize Citations & Provide Exact Provisions:**
   - Deliver clear citations with direct statutory quotes.
