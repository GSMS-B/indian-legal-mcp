# Healthcare & Food Standards Framework

- **FSSA Section 26:** Responsibilities of food business operators to ensure food satisfies safety standards.
- **FSSA Section 59:** Penalties for unsafe food ranging up to life imprisonment and ₹10 Lakhs fine if death results.
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act Section 17A & 17B:** Definitions of adulterated and spurious drugs.
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act Section 27:** Penalties for manufacture or sale of spurious or adulterated drugs.
- **Mental Healthcare Act Section 115:** Decriminalization of suicide attempt under IPC/BNS for persons with mental illness.
