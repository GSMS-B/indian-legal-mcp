# Environmental Compliance & NGT Jurisdiction

- **EPA Section 3 & 5:** Powers of Central Government to protect environment and issue closure directions to industries.
- **NGT Act Section 14:** NGT has jurisdiction over all civil cases where a substantial question relating to environment arises.
- **NGT Act Section 15:** Power of NGT to award relief and compensation to victims of pollution and for restitution of damaged property.
- **NGT Act Section 20:** Mandatory application of Precautionary Principle and Polluter Pays Principle in all adjudications.
