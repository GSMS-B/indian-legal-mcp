## 5-Step Execution Workflow
1. **Identify the Core Employment Question:**
   - Wage/Remuneration Dispute: Governed by `COW`.
   - Social Security (PF/ESI/Maternity/Gratuity): Governed by `CSS`.
   - Strike/Lockout/Layoff/Closure: Governed by `IRC`.
   - Safety, Overtime, Leave: Governed by `OSH`.
   - Sexual Harassment Complaint: Governed by `POSH`.
2. **Review Code Consolidation Architecture:**
   - Consult `reference/labor_code_transition.md` to see which older statutes were subsumed into the 4 Labor Codes.
3. **Verify Mandatory Committee & Compliance Mandates:**
   - Under `POSH` Section 4: Mandatory constitution of Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) for every workplace with 10+ employees.
   - Under `CSS` Section 53: Continuous service of 5 years required for gratuity (reduced to 1 year for fixed-term employees).
4. **Check Closure & Retrenchment Thresholds:**
   - Under `IRC` Chapter IX: Requirement of prior government permission for layoff, retrenchment, or closure applies to establishments with 300+ workers.
5. **Synthesize Citations & Provide Exact Provisions:**
   - Deliver clear citations with direct statutory quotes.
