# POSH Act, 2013 Compliance Workflow

- **Section 4:** Mandatory Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) presided over by a senior woman employee, with minimum 50% women members and an external NGO/legal expert.
- **Section 9:** Time limit of 3 months from the incident for filing a written complaint.
- **Section 11:** Inquiry must be completed within 90 days.
- **Section 13:** Employer must act on ICC inquiry report within 60 days.
