# The 4 Unified Labour Codes Architecture

The 4 Labour Codes consolidate 29 legacy central labour enactments:

1. **Code on Wages, 2019 (`COW`):** Subsumes Payment of Wages Act 1936, Minimum Wages Act 1948, Payment of Bonus Act 1965, and Equal Remuneration Act 1976.
2. **Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (`IRC`):** Subsumes Trade Unions Act 1926, Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act 1946, and Industrial Disputes Act 1947.
3. **Code on Social Security, 2020 (`CSS`):** Subsumes EPF Act 1952, ESI Act 1948, Maternity Benefit Act 1961, Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, etc.
4. **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (`OSH`):** Subsumes Factories Act 1948, Mines Act 1952, Contract Labour Act 1970, etc.
